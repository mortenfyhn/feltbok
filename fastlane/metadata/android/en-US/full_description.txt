Feltbok is an app to quickly log Norwegian bird sightings in the field and export them to Artsobservasjoner.

Features:

* Quick entry of observations in the field
* Easy export to Artsobservasjoner
* Choose locality on a map
* Fetch your private localities from Artsobservasjoner
* Create new private localities on the map
* Works without a network connection

Feltbok is free and open source (AGPL-3.0). The app is made for use in Norway, and the interface is Norwegian only.
