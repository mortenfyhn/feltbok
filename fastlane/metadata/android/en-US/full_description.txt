Feltbok is an app to quickly log bird sightings in the field, and later export them to Artsobservasjoner. Does not require logging in or an internet connection.

Features:
<ul>
<li>Quick entry of observations in the field</li>
<li>Easy export to Artsobservasjoner</li>
<li>Optimized species lookup: type one letter to find your bird</li>
<li>Map view to choose site</li>
<li>Fetch your private sites from Artsobservasjoner</li>
<li>Create new private sites</li>
</ul>

Feltbok is made for use in Norway, and the interface is in Norwegian only.
