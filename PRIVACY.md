# Feltbok Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Feltbok is an app for recording bird observations. It has no user account, no ads, and no
analytics or tracking. The developer runs no server, and does not receive, store or share
any of your data.

## Your observations stay on your phone

Everything you record is saved in the app's own private storage, which other apps cannot
read. It is never uploaded. To get your observations into Artsobservasjoner you copy them
as text and paste them in yourself.

## The backup file in your Downloads folder

Each time you save, the app also writes a copy of your observations as a text file to
`Downloads/Feltbok/feltbok-sikkerhetskopi.tsv`. This is deliberate: if the app ever fails,
your notes from the field are still recoverable with any file manager.

Two things follow from that file being in shared storage rather than the app's private
storage. Other apps that can read your files can read it. And it stays on the phone after
you uninstall Feltbok — uninstalling removes the app's private storage, but not this file.
Delete it yourself if you don't want it kept.

## Location

The app uses your location to show where you are on the map and to find nearby localities.
It is used on the phone only, and is not sent anywhere. You can use the app without granting
location access.

## Other services the app contacts

- **OpenStreetMap**, for map tiles. Like any web service, it can see your IP address.
- **artsobservasjoner.no**, in two cases:
  - When you look at the locality map, the app asks for the public localities in the area shown,
    so that localities created recently also appear. Artsobservasjoner can then see roughly where
    on the map you are looking, and your IP address. Nothing about your observations is sent.
  - If you use "Synk mine lokaliteter". You log in on Artsobservasjoner's own website, shown inside
    the app. The app never reads or stores your password.

  What you send to Artsobservasjoner is covered by their privacy policy.
